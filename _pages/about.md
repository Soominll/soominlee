I am a <b>Bioinformatics Ph.D. student at UC San Diego</b> and a <b>Graduate Student Researcher</b> in the <a href='https://einav.lji.org/' target="_blank">Laboratory of Tal Einav</a> at the <a href='https://www.lji.org/' target="_blank">La Jolla Institute for Immunology (LJI)</a>. My research integrates `dynamics modeling`, `multi-omics`, and `machine learning` to map early-life immunity, antibody kinetics, and viral infection/vaccination dynamics.

Prior to joining UCSD and LJI, I developed computational pipelines for precision oncology at <a href='https://inocras.com/' target="_blank">Inocras</a>, engineering structural variant algorithms, deep mutation callers, and clinical knowledgebase integration tools. I earned an M.S. in Bio and Brain Engineering from Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)—where I developed GAN- and GNN-based computational methods to combat cancer drug resistance ([link to paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38034356/) & U.S./Korean patents)—and a B.S. in Computer Science and Life Science from Handong Global University (HGU), graduating <i>summa cum laude</i>.

<b>My primary research interests include:</b>
1. <b>Understanding Early-Life Immunity & Antibody Kinetics:</b> Modeling maternal antibody waning and infant antibody dynamics following vaccination and viral infection to optimize early-life immunization timing ([medRxiv preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.04.26352413v1.full), under review at <i>Nature Immunology</i>).
2. <b>Optimizing Early-Life Diagnostics:</b> Evaluating multi-assay diagnostic frameworks (HAI titers, multiplexed MSD IgG/IgA, anti-NP, and RT-PCR) to define accurate and cost-effective infection surveillance strategies.
3. <b>Mapping Proteome-Wide Immunity:</b> Utilizing high-throughput phage display immunoprecipitation sequencing (PhIP-seq) to characterize peptide-level antibody specificity changes across viral exposures.
